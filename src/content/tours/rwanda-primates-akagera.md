Rwanda is small, so you can see savanna, rainforest and volcanoes in one week.

## Savanna, forest and volcanoes

Start with game drives in **Akagera**, where lions and rhinos have been reintroduced. Cross the country to **Nyungwe Forest** for chimpanzee tracking and the canopy walkway, then finish with gorilla trekking in **Volcanoes National Park**.

## Make it yours

Add Lake Kivu, golden monkeys, or a second gorilla trek.

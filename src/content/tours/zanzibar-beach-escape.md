Zanzibar mixes history and beach in one easy island. This trip starts in the old town and ends with your feet in the sand.

## Stone Town and the coast

Walk the lanes of **Stone Town** with a local guide, visit a spice farm and eat at the Forodhani night market. Then head to the coast for three nights of swimming, snorkelling trips and dhow sailing at sunset.

## Make it yours

Choose a lively or quiet coast, add Prison Island or a dolphin trip, or extend with Pemba or Mafia.

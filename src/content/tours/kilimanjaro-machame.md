Kilimanjaro is the highest mountain in Africa and the tallest free-standing mountain in the world, and you can walk to the top. The Machame Route is one of the most beautiful ways up, passing through rainforest, heath, alpine desert and the glaciers near the summit.

## The climb

Seven days on the mountain follow the principle of climbing high and sleeping low, which gives your body time to adjust to the altitude. Summit night starts around midnight, so you reach **Uhuru Peak** (5,895 m) near sunrise before descending to the forest.

## Before and after

You meet your guide in **Arusha** for a kit check and briefing, and celebrate the climb with a night in **Moshi**. Many climbers add a few days of safari or time on Zanzibar to recover.

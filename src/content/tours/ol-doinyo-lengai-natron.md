Ol Doinyo Lengai is an active volcano above Lake Natron. The climb is short but very steep.

## The climb

Start around midnight and climb for five to seven hours on loose ash to reach the crater for sunrise. After resting, walk to the **Engare Sero waterfall** and see the flamingos on **Lake Natron**.

## Make it yours

Combine with a Serengeti safari, or add a night to rest before the climb.

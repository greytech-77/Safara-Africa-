This is Tanzania at its most private. You travel with your own guide and vehicle, stay in small camps placed for the best wildlife, and fly out at the end instead of driving back.

## How the journey unfolds

Start in **Tarangire**, where elephant herds gather under the baobabs and the private areas allow walking safaris and night drives. Two nights on the **Ngorongoro** highlands give a full, unhurried day in the crater. The journey ends with four nights in the **Serengeti**, including a sunrise balloon flight, in a camp chosen to follow the migration in your month.

## Shaped around you

The route above is a starting point. Add a bush dinner, a private photographic vehicle, a stay on Zanzibar or a Kilimanjaro climb. Tell us how you like to travel and we will rebuild the trip around it.

Volcanoes National Park is about a three-hour drive from Kigali, which makes Rwanda one of the quickest places to see mountain gorillas.

## The trek

After a briefing at Kinigi, rangers lead you into the bamboo and forest on the slopes of the Virunga volcanoes. Treks take from one to several hours, and you spend one hour with a habituated gorilla family.

## Make it yours

Add golden monkey tracking, the hike to Dian Fossey's grave, or a stay at Lake Kivu.

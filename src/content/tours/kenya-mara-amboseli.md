A first Kenya safari in one week, with three very different parks.

## The route

Watch elephants in **Amboseli** with Kilimanjaro behind them, look for rhino around **Lake Nakuru**, then spend two days on the plains of the **Maasai Mara**, where the migration herds usually arrive between July and October.

## Make it yours

Add a night, fly back to Nairobi from the Mara, or cross into Tanzania for the Serengeti.

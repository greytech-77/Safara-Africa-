Two contrasting regions linked by short flights.

## North and south

**Samburu**'s dry country is home to species found only in the north, such as Grevy's zebra and reticulated giraffe. Then fly to a camp in a **Maasai Mara** conservancy, where vehicle numbers are limited and night drives and walks are possible.

## Make it yours

Add Nairobi's Giraffe Centre, a balloon flight over the Mara, or a beach stay in Diani or Zanzibar.

The classic way to see Tanzania is overland on the northern circuit, watching the landscape change from baobab country to the Rift Valley, the open plains and the rim of an ancient volcano.

## The route

Start among the elephant herds of **Tarangire**, then drop to the forest and soda lake of **Lake Manyara**. Three nights in the **Serengeti** give time to follow the big cats and, depending on the month, the migration herds. The trip ends with a full day on the floor of the **Ngorongoro** Crater before driving back to Arusha.

## Adjusting the pace

Add nights in the Serengeti, a hot-air balloon flight, a Maasai village visit or a few days on Zanzibar. We plan the details so you can enjoy the drive.

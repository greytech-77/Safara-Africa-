Mount Meru rises above Arusha and is often climbed as preparation for Kilimanjaro.

## The trek

From Momella Gate you walk with an armed ranger past giraffe and buffalo to **Miriakamba Hut**, then up to **Saddle Hut**. The summit night follows a narrow ridge on the rim of the crater, with sunrise views across to Kilimanjaro.

## Make it yours

Shorten to three days, or follow with a Kilimanjaro climb or a safari.

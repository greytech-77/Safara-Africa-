The Lemosho Route starts in the remote western forest and joins the Machame Route on the Shira Plateau. The extra day gives your body more time to adjust.

## The climb

Walk through rainforest and moorland to the **Shira Plateau**, climb high to Lava Tower and sleep lower at Barranco, then traverse below the southern glaciers to Barafu Camp for the summit night to **Uhuru Peak**.

## Make it yours

Add a ninth day on the mountain, a pre-climb night at a farm lodge, or a safari and Zanzibar afterwards.

After early starts and dusty game drives, a few days on Zanzibar is the natural end to a Tanzanian safari. Flights from the Serengeti airstrips, Arusha and Kilimanjaro take between one and two hours.

## Stone Town

Zanzibar's old town is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, built over centuries by Swahili, Omani, Indian and European traders. Spend at least half a day on a guided walk past the carved wooden doors, the old fort and the markets, and stay for the evening food market at Forodhani Gardens.

## Choosing a coast

- **North (Nungwi and Kendwa):** swimming at all tides and lively sunsets.
- **East (Paje, Matemwe, Pongwe):** quieter beaches and reefs, with big tides that are good for kitesurfing.
- **South-west (Fumba, Kizimkazi):** small, peaceful hotels and dolphin trips.

## Beyond the beach

Visit a spice farm, sail on a traditional dhow at sunset, snorkel at Mnemba Atoll, or walk in Jozani Forest to see the endemic red colobus monkey.

## How long to stay

Three or four nights is enough to rest, see Stone Town and spend time on the water. Honeymooners often stay longer.

Nyerere National Park, once part of the Selous Game Reserve, is one of the largest protected areas in Africa and a short flight from Zanzibar. It is the easiest way to add real wilderness to a beach holiday.

## Two days in the wild

Game drives look for elephant, lion, buffalo, giraffe and African wild dog. A boat safari on the **Rufiji River** brings you close to hippos, crocodiles and a huge variety of birds, and some camps also offer guided walks.

## Make it yours

Stay a third night, add a fly-camping night, or swap Nyerere for Mikumi or the Serengeti.

Uganda is one of the few places where you can track both chimpanzees and mountain gorillas on one short trip.

## Chimps, then gorillas

Start with chimpanzee tracking in **Kibale Forest**, known for the variety of its primates. Then drive south to **Bwindi Impenetrable National Park**, home to around half of the world's mountain gorillas, for a full day of gorilla trekking.

## Make it yours

Add a boat trip on the Kazinga Channel in Queen Elizabeth National Park, rest days at Lake Bunyonyi, or flights to save driving time.

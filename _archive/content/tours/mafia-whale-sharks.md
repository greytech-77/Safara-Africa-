Mafia is the quiet one among Tanzania's islands: no big resorts, a protected marine park and some of the best diving on the coast.

## In and under the water

Dive or snorkel the reefs of **Mafia Island Marine Park**, explore Chole Island by dhow, and, from roughly October to March, swim with the whale sharks that feed off Kilindoni.

## Make it yours

Add a stay on Thanda Island, combine with a fly-in safari to Nyerere, or add a diving course.

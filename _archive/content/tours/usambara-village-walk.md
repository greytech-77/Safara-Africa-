The Usambara Mountains are green, cool and full of small farming villages. Walks are gentle and led by local guides.

## The walks

Hike to the **Irente Viewpoint** for views over the Maasai Steppe, walk through farms and villages, and visit the **Magamba Forest**, home to black-and-white colobus monkeys.

## Make it yours

Extend to a longer walk towards Mtae, or continue to the coast or Zanzibar.

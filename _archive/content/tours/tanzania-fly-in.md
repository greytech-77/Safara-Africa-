Tanzania's parks are far apart, and from the air it makes sense. This journey links the busiest and the wildest of them by scheduled bush flights, then finishes on the Indian Ocean.

## North, south and the coast

Begin in the **Serengeti**, placed where the migration is expected in your month of travel. Fly south to **Ruaha**, a vast and little-visited park known for big lion prides, wild dog and elephants along the Great Ruaha River. End with three nights on **Zanzibar**, with time for Stone Town or simply the beach.

## Make it yours

Swap Ruaha for Nyerere National Park, add a balloon flight, or extend the beach stay. Everything on this itinerary can be adjusted.

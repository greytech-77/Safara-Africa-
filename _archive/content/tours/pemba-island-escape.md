Pemba sits north of Zanzibar and sees only a fraction of its visitors. Hills of clove trees run down to mangroves, quiet beaches and some of the most dramatic reef walls in East Africa.

## Island life

Dive the walls of the **Pemba Channel**, snorkel from a dhow, visit clove farms and villages, and walk in Ngezi Forest.

## Make it yours

Combine with Zanzibar for a two-island holiday, or add a safari before.
